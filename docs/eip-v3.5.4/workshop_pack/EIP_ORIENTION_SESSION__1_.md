## Key Outcomes

The orientation introduced the EIP 2026 program — an official initiative of the Global Entrepreneurship Festival (GEF) — to ~4,000–5,000 applicants representing ~30 nationalities.  All attendees are on full scholarship with no ticket purchase required. The program culminates in a live pitching event at the JW Marriott Hotel, Cairo, on 6–8 November 2026, where top 3 businesses share a $35,000 seed investment prize.

## Announcements

- **Program purpose:** Identify, train, mentor, and connect high-potential startups with investors and global markets.
- **Strategic partners:** E-village (funding top 3 businesses); Shark Tank Egypt (judging panel).
- **Prize structure:** Top 3 share $35,000 seed investment; #1 winner receives a fully paid international business trip.
- **YSR (Youth Social Responsibility)** confirmed as a collaborating partner, aligning with this year's theme of funding ideas and lifting people.

## Program Stages (Road to Pitch)

1. **Virtual Orientation** — current session
2. **Investor Readiness Workshop** — compulsory; covers pitch deck, financials, problem framing
3. **Pitch Deck Submission** — deadline: **30 October**
4. **Expert Assessment** — decks reviewed; selected founders invited for further grooming
5. **Masterclass & Startup Clinic** — at the festival
6. **Round 1 Pitch** — live judging at GEF
7. **Final Assessment & Selection**
8. **Final Pitch** — finalists only
9. **Winners Announced** — top 3 revealed on **8 November 2026**

## Key Dates

- **21 & 24 October** — Hybrid investment readiness training (in-person for Cairo; virtual option available)
- **30 October** — Pitch deck submission deadline
- **6–8 November** — GEF / EIP live event, JW Marriott Hotel, Cairo
- **8 November** — Certificate presentation and winner announcement

## What Attendees Receive

- Full EIP scholarship (no cost to attend)
- **+1 complimentary EIP ticket** for a co-founder, spouse, or support person
- Co-branded certificate (attendance on 8 November is mandatory to receive it)
- Access to investors, judges, mentors, and global network
