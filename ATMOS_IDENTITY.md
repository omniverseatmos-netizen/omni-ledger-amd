# AtMoS identity

**AtMoS** = **Automated Traceability & Movement Operations System**.

AtMoS is the public product layer under OmNiVeRsE. `OmNi-Ledger Deterministic Control Core` remains the internal deterministic engine and evidence layer.

The name intentionally avoids `AtMoS`, which is already strongly associated with Microsoft Sentinel, a cloud-native SIEM/security product.

Descriptor: **AI-assisted intake, deterministic inventory control, and inspectable evidence.**
